The stage

What happens here

Sonic Horizon carries the opening ceremony, and from the moment the first set starts the floor never closes again — through Friday night, all of Saturday, and on to Sunday afternoon.

The opening

Ceremony at quarter to four on Friday. Everything else on this stage follows from it.

Through the nights

Psytrance in its full range, from the driving and full-on to the dark and hypnotic, held from dusk to well past dawn.

The linkup

The gathering reaches this dancefloor for the Prayer for Peace — the fifteen minutes shared with every Earthdance on Earth.

The close

Sunday afternoon brings the last sets of the weekend, and the floor finally falls quiet.
