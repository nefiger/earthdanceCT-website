The stage

What happens here

Mellow Meadow opens the festival at noon on Friday and is still going on Sunday afternoon. It is the stage where the tempo follows the light.

By day

Chilled sets, live instruments and acoustic sessions, with the workshops and ceremony that give the daylight hours their shape.

After dark

The meadow hands over. A techno takeover, then drum & bass, carrying both nights through to sunrise.

The mornings

Yoga at nine on Saturday and Sunday, tai chi, and a movement session played live. The part of the weekend that happens nowhere else.

The sacred fire

A central fire burns here all weekend. It is where the procession gathers before the linkup.
