Crew Makes the Gathering

The hands that hold the space

Long before the gates open, and long after the last set ends, Earthdance runs on people doing the unglamorous, essential work — kitchens, gates, parking, and the thousand small things a gathering needs to hold together. Meet some of the crew bringing 2026 to life.

Photo: Jaco Brewer Photography

Leading the Return

The people steering it home

Behind the crew, a small team carries Earthdance Cape Town year-round — the people who brought the festival back in 2025 and are doing it again for 2026.

Festival Custodian & Lead

Michael "Birdman" Aldridge

Birdman has spent decades at the meeting point of trance, consciousness and collective transformation, from Johannesburg's early techno raves through years of ceremony at Altered States to Earthdance's 1999 gathering in Dharamsala. When the previous organisers stepped back, he secured the licence from Earthdance Global and called the community together to bring the festival home in 2025, and continues as its custodian and lead for 2026.

Music & Programme

Mikey Dredd

Mikey came up in South Africa's late-90s and early-2000s psytrance scene, remembered for the marathon sets that helped define those early outdoor gatherings. He joined Earthdance production in 2008 and has worked across stage, sound and operations ever since — this year he's shaping the 2026 programme and co-hosting Frequency Check alongside Birdman.

Production Manager

Jennifer Walker

Jennifer is Earthdance Cape Town's production manager for 2026, an event producer and systems thinker with 25 years spent between complex systems and the people who use them. Her focus is making the festival sustainable without losing what makes it matter — building the conditions for people to arrive as co-creators, not just an audience.
