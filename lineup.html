Heart and Beat of Humanity · 18–20 September 2026

The lineup

Forty-nine artists across two stages at Kromrivier Farm: psytrance running through the nights at Sonic Horizon, and a Mellow Meadow that changes character after dark. More live and acoustic artists are still to come.

How the Weekend Sounds

Two stages, three distinct sounds

Earthdance is not a single dancefloor with a single sound. The weekend moves between two stages, and one of them changes character completely after dark.

Sonic Horizon is the kinetic heart of the festival: psytrance, from the first sets through to sunrise. Mellow Meadow is the softer, community-held space by day — workshops, ceremony, slower moments and the central sacred fire — and then hands itself over after dark, first to a techno takeover and then to drum & bass.

Set times will be published closer to the event. Until then, this is who is playing.

Sonic Horizon

The kinetic heart of the weekend

Sonic Horizon carries the festival's stronger pulse: psytrance from the early sets through to the sunrise, and the stage most people mean when they talk about the Earthdance dancefloor. It is also where the global Prayer for Peace lands at 01:00 on Saturday, when the music stops and the whole gathering links up with Earthdance communities around the world.

Mellow Meadow

Soft by day, taken over by night

Through the daylight hours Mellow Meadow is the gentler side of the gathering — workshops, ceremony, slower moments and the central sacred fire. After dark it changes character entirely, handing the space first to a techno takeover and then to drum & bass. Same stretch of grass, a completely different night.
