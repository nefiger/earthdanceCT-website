Heart and Beat of Humanity · 18–20 September 2026

The lineup

The lineup so far, across two stages at Kromrivier Farm: psytrance running through the nights at Sonic Horizon, and a Mellow Meadow that changes character after dark. More live and acoustic artists are still to come.

How the Weekend Sounds

Two stages, no two hours the same

Earthdance is not a single dancefloor with a single sound. The weekend moves between two stages, and one of them changes character completely after dark.

Sonic Horizon is the kinetic heart of the festival: psytrance, from the first sets through to sunrise. Mellow Meadow holds the ceremony, the workshops and the sacred fire through the day — and then gets taken over, first by techno and then by drum & bass.

Set times land here as soon as the running order is locked. Until then, this is who is playing.

Sonic Horizon

The kinetic heart of the weekend

Sonic Horizon carries the festival's stronger pulse: psytrance from the early sets through to the sunrise, and the stage most people mean when they talk about the Earthdance dancefloor. It is also where the global Prayer for Peace lands at 01:00 on Saturday, when the music stops and the whole gathering links up with Earthdance communities around the world.

Mellow Meadow

The meadow doesn't stay mellow

Through the daylight hours this is where the gathering does its deepest work — workshops, ceremony, and the central sacred fire that burns all weekend. Then the light goes and the stage is handed over: a techno takeover first, then drum & bass until the early hours. Same stretch of grass, an entirely different night.
