## Design Context

### Users

Earthdance Cape Town attendees and prospective attendees, usually planning on a phone and often close to the event date. They need to understand what the gathering is, decide whether it is for them, buy tickets, plan transport and accommodation, and arrive prepared for a multi-day camping festival at Kromrivier Farm.

### Brand Personality

Soulful, communal and practical. The voice is warm and human, rooted in peace, music, participation, sustainability and care for the land. It should feel like a real gathering made by people, never like corporate festival marketing.

### Aesthetic Direction

An immersive, night-sky festival atmosphere using Earthdance's established deep indigo surfaces, vivid warm-to-cool colour language, distinctive Danube display typography and documentary event photography. Layouts should feel energetic and expressive while keeping practical information calm, legible and easy to act on. Preserve the existing dark visual system rather than introducing generic campaign cards, dashboard patterns or social-poster layouts.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the attendee's next decision: understand, book or prepare.
2. Keep operational facts in accessible HTML; use photography for atmosphere and authenticity rather than embedding essential details in images.
3. Use strong hierarchy and generous spacing so urgent information scans cleanly on mobile.
4. Preserve Earthdance's soul through direct, human language and real community imagery.
5. Treat Practical Info as the source of truth, with concise routes into it from higher-traffic pages.
