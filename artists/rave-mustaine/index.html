About Rave Mustaine

A mysterious figure moving through the darker corners of the South African and Mozambican underground, Rave Mustaine brings dark psytech and hypnotic frequencies designed to bend minds and rearrange dancefloors.

He has appeared in places nobody remembers, at hours nobody should have been awake, leaving behind little more than distorted memories and suspiciously heavy basslines. Come find out what happens when Rave Mustaine comes out to play.
