Food & Drink

Atma Vegan and vegetarian food with close on 20 years on the festival circuit — curries, roti, samosas, sweets and now barista coffee too. C Chai'la A zero-proof bar of chai, Chinese tea and cacao, with dairy-free options. Fresh Bean Coffee Company Espresso-based coffee, matcha, fresh juices and boba, plus milkshakes, slushes and dirty sodas. Love Pizza A family-run wood-fired pizza institution at Cape Town festivals since 2009 — part of the Earthdance family for over a decade. Munchies Roti wraps, toasties and hand-cut waffle fries — organic, coconut-oil-fried comfort food to fuel the dancefloor. Mushmore A non-alcoholic wellness bar for whatever mood you're in — uplift and energise, replenish and revitalise, or wind down and socialise. Oh So Peachy Delicious food and raw juices made with love — pancakes, samosas, cold-pressed juices and heartier plates.

Craft & Goods

Afrikan Crown Rasta clothing and accessories — trance dresses, reggae tees and vests, and smoking accessories. F Firehawk Ink Tattoos — take a piece of Earthdance home under your skin. GeoVinty Clothing A celebration of sacred geometry, creativity and individuality — each garment upcycled with bleach into a unique piece of wearable art. Rosa Divina Adornment Intentional handmade adornment — handwoven talismans and ritual wear devoted to radiance, sovereignty and self-empowerment. Wild & Woven For the wanderers, the wild ones, the barefoot dancers and the beautifully unconventional — 100% natural-fibre handmade clothing.

Wellness

Blissful Wellness Escape The ultimate wellness escape — massage, henna art, hair braiding and a glitter bar. Close your eyes, take a breath, restore, glow. Chill and Charge A relaxed space to power up your phone — and yourself — between sets. Groove Gear A festival lifestyle lounge for LED and UV accessories, face and body gems, hair braiding, glow transformations and relaxing massage. Lekker Leaf Wellness Cannabis and wellness body goods — "where community grows one leaf at a time."
