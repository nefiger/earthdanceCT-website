Meet the traders

Food, drink, craft, clothing and wellness — the people bringing the market village to life at Kromrivier Farm.

Photo: Groove Gear

Food & Drink

Munchies Roti wraps, toasties and hand-cut waffle fries — organic, coconut-oil-fried comfort food to fuel the dancefloor. Mushmore An adaptogen and nootropic wellness bar — mood-enhancing mocktails, functional mushroom coffees and social tonics. C Chai'la A zero-proof bar of chai, Chinese tea and cacao, with dairy-free options. Fresh Bean Coffee Company Espresso-based coffee, matcha, fresh juices and boba, plus milkshakes, slushes and dirty sodas. L Love Pizza Large wood-fired-style pizzas, from a classic Margherita to vegan and gluten-free options. O Oh So Peachy Pancakes, smoothies, raw juices, stir-fry and Thai curry — quick festival food with a peachy attitude. A Atma Vegan and vegetarian food, coffees and smoothies, with a gluten-free option.

Craft & Goods

GeoVinty Clothing A celebration of sacred geometry, creativity and individuality — each garment upcycled with bleach into a unique piece of wearable art. R Rosa Divina Adornment Jewellery and accessories, from small pieces to statement adornment. Wild & Woven For the wanderers, the wild ones, the barefoot dancers and the beautifully unconventional — 100% natural-fibre handmade clothing. A Afrikan Crown Rasta-inspired clothing and accessories. F Firehawk Ink Tattoos — take a piece of Earthdance home under your skin.

Wellness

Groove Gear A festival lifestyle lounge for LED and UV accessories, face and body gems, hair braiding, glow transformations and relaxing massage. Lekker Leaf Wellness Cannabis and wellness body goods — "where community grows one leaf at a time." C Chill and Charge A charging and chill-out space to power up your phone — and yourself — between sets. B Blissful Wellness Escape Massage treatments, hair braiding and a glitter bar — a moment of calm between dancefloors.

Earthdance Cape Town is part of a worldwide movement for peace, connection and collective celebration.
